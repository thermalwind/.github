## The idea

A company holds three things: **knowledge** (what it knows), **data** (what it holds) and
**programs** (what it does). All three live **local-first** — on machines the company owns,
in open files it controls — with any server in a supporting role, never the source of truth.

An **agent** reads the knowledge, works the data and runs the programs. What it produces is
a **draft, never the truth**. A person works it over: a finished document graduates to
**Source**, everything else goes back in as knowledge or data, where the next round builds
on it. Two things no tool may touch: the **originals**, and the **design** the system runs by.

## What it is not

- Not a note-taking app, and not a chat with a model.
- Not a replacement for the systems of record you already run — it sits between your
  material and an agent.
- Not a hosted service. If it stops being yours, it has stopped being this.
